                    HOW VIGIL WORKS

                    Clear decisions. Verifiable reasons.

                    Security checks should help you decide what to do next, without asking you to trust a black box.

                    01

                    Your content stays here

                    Messages and page source are processed by the local VIGIL service. Submitted URLs are inspected as text, never opened.

                    02

                    Rules make the decision

                    Visible evidence drives ALLOW, WARN, or DENY. The optional local model may add a warning, but cannot clear a rule-based denial.

                    03

                    Actions stay yours

                    Agent checks evaluate a proposed action. VIGIL never sends a message, shares data, opens a link, or pays on your behalf.
